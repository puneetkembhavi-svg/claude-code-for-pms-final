Coverage — recovering after a quiet stretch

Four real responders from the 4.2 data, each with their own handler. Nothing new was added to any of these screens — watch what happens to the same four cards under today's system versus the proposed fix.

How this works ▾
Since 4.2, missing a call by a few seconds is scored the same as refusing it — and there's no way for that to heal on its own. A responder who has one unlucky week gets asked less, which gives them fewer chances to prove themselves, which keeps their score down. The fix: let a quiet stretch drift back toward normal on its own, the longer it goes untouched — no new setting, nothing a handler has to learn.
